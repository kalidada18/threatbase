# Surface brief: Investigate (/investigate)

Scope: Deep Investigation dossier; mode Persuade (Pro buyers evaluate in one lookup; existing Pro users triage here).
Audience & job: evaluate/trust a verdict on one IOC and act (block, whitelist, subscribe).
Proof: real per-source evidence, relations graph, 90-day calendar, AI narrative.
Constraints: VT/OpenRouter reliability under repair; failed/skipped/no-record are first-class honest states, never filler; tag wire format frozen; no em-dashes; lucide icons; WCAG floors kept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dossier is a freshly-lifted raku vessel: evidence glazes over soot, and the page owns an asymmetric kiln-record composition at analysis scale. It refuses the centered search-hero with a tabbed evidence console (the category default) and refuses uniform grid symmetry.

OWN-WORLD: Soot-black carbonized-clay ground (#17140f); crackled silver-white glaze panels (#d8d2c4) with dark ink; ember orange (#ff6a2b) reserved for live interaction: the commit control and any focused control (touch=heat), never severity; iron-slip red (≈#a4432c) carries flagged/malicious, copper-flash green (≈#5f8a72) carries clean; ash (#6e675c) carries skipped/failed/secondary. Quiet grotesque (Manrope) reads, mono (JetBrains) counts; kiln-log stacked stage labels ("STAGE 04 · TRACE") with hairline leader rules; hand-drawn fracture marks instead of glass cards and gradients.

STORY: Visitor submits an IOC, the kiln bed of source tiles settles stage by stage, verdict scale reads first (biggest shard, off-axis), identity and narrative flank it, the graph gets the widest band, and every section header is a numbered firing stage so the reader believes the dossier was made by a deliberate process, then acts.

FIRST VIEWPORT (empty state): off-axis vessel: search field + headline placed left of center at oversized scale on soot, kiln-log stage list stacked right of center as the proof column; the single ember Investigate button sits on the vessel's axis, nothing centered. (report state): verdict slab 5/12 left carrying status, source-count line, confidence annotation with leader rule, and the source kiln-bed; identity shard 3/12; narrative slab 4/12; rows never symmetric.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
